About us

Building Foundations for Lifelong Learning and Achievement

The Journey of VK Bansal

VK Bansal, born on October 26, 1949, in the Jhansi district of Uttar Pradesh, was a visionary educator who revolutionized the way students prepared for competitive exams in India. Raised in a household that valued hard work and discipline, VK Bansal pursued his education at the Indian Institute of Technology, Banaras Hindu University. After graduation, he worked at J.K. Synthetics in Kota, where his passion for education and helping students started to take shape.

In 1981, VK Bansal recognized a gap in the guidance available for JEE aspirants. Determined to bridge this gap, he established Bansal Classes, which quickly became a pioneer in JEE coaching. His innovative teaching methods, unwavering dedication, and positive attitude led the institution to produce exceptional results, setting new benchmarks in academic excellence.

VK Bansal believed that accepting change and creating strategies accordingly are key qualities of a leader. This belief became the foundation of Bansal Classes, which has continuously evolved its methodologies to suit the dynamic needs of students. His legacy continues to inspire both educators and students across India.

To reach heights, one must start climbing, and if the journey is difficult, perseverance is the key to success.
